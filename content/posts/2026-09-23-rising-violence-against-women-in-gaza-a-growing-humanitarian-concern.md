Reports from the United Nations Population Fund (UNFPA) indicate a troubling increase in violence against women and girls in Gaza, highlighting a critical humanitarian situation in the region. The UNFPA's report reveals a 123% increase in reported cases of early and forced marriages in the second quarter of 2026 compared to the first quarter.

## Escalating Gender-Based Violence

The report links the rise in early marriages to deteriorating conditions within Gaza. The ongoing crisis, which has been exacerbated by hostilities between Israel and Hamas since 2023, has severely impacted social structures and economic stability, escalating risks for women.

Nestor Owomuhangi, the UNFPA representative to Palestine, highlights the breakdown of social order and rising poverty as key drivers of gender-based violence. He notes that these factors force some families into considering early marriage as a desperate survival strategy.

## The Reality of Early and Forced Marriages

Girls as young as 12 are being married off as families struggle with economic hardships. This practice, seen as a means of protection or economic gain, poses significant risks to young girls, depriving them of childhood and education while perpetuating poverty and dependency cycles.

## Challenges in Data and Diverse Perspectives

Gathering accurate data remains challenging due to variations in local customs and societal norms concerning marriage and gender roles. These variations can influence both the reporting and perception of the increase in violence and early marriages.
