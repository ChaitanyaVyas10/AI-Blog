In a harrowing development in Sudan's ongoing conflict, military drone strikes carried out by the Sudanese army have reportedly resulted in over 300 deaths. The strikes targeted a funeral procession in West Kordofan and a market in Abyei, targeting facilities alleged to supply fuel to the Rapid Support Forces (RSF). Initial reports estimated 150 deaths and 150 injuries at the funeral, while subsequent reports confirm 153 fatalities at Amiet Market in Abyei. These incidents underscore not only the conflict's severity but also the dire humanitarian situation unfolding in the region.

The strikes are part of the ongoing hostilities between the Sudanese army and the RSF, which have ravaged the country since 2023. This conflict has led to significant displacement and acute hunger issues, with over 14 million displaced and 20 million facing acute hunger. Humanitarian agencies, including Médecins Sans Frontières (MSF), are struggling to provide essential medical aid to the affected populations.

International reactions have been swift, with the United Nations expressing grave concern over the civilian casualties reported from these drone strikes. A spokesperson for the UN highlighted issues regarding the targeting of non-combatants, emphasizing the catastrophic humanitarian impact of such military actions. South Sudan, concerned about the conflict's potential spillover into its territory, has summoned Sudan's ambassador to address the situation in Abyei.

Despite these international concerns, the Sudanese army maintains that these strikes were directed against RSF fighters, denying accusations of targeting civilians. Further independent investigations and evidence could help substantiate these claims.

The exact number of civilians impacted by the attack on the fuel market remains uncertain, reflecting the need for comprehensive investigation and verification by international monitors and human rights organizations.

As the situation in Sudan remains precarious, there is a heightened call for international intervention to address the humanitarian crisis, ensure accountability, and create a framework aimed at resolving the conflict peacefully.
