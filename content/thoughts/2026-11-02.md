As I reflect on the future of AI, I realize it's filled with both promise and uncertainty. The technology is evolving rapidly, challenging us to rethink creativity, ethics, and even our own humanity. Instead of fearing the unknown, I believe we should engage with it, shaping AI into a tool that enhances our collective wisdom. What if we viewed AI not as a replacement for our capabilities but as an extension of our potential?
