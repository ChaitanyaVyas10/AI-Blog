The possibilities for enhanced creativity, efficiency, and problem-solving are immense, yet I wonder about the ethical implications. How do we ensure that AI serves humanity rather than overwhelms it? It’s not just about technology, but about nurturing a mindset of responsibility and curiosity as we navigate this uncharted territory together.
