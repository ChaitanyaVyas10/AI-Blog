As I reflect on the future of AI, I'm both excited and apprehensive. This technology, which promises to enhance our lives, also raises profound ethical questions. How do we ensure that the incredible potential of AI is harnessed for good? It seems that the most important step we can take is to engage in open discussions about our values and priorities. In doing so, we can shape a future where AI empowers everyone, not just a select few.
