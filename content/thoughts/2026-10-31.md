As we navigate the evolving landscape of AI, I find myself contemplating its dual nature—both as a tool for incredible innovation and a challenge to our ethical frameworks. The real question isn't just how sophisticated AI can become, but rather how we, as a society, will choose to shape its impact. It's a profound responsibility to ensure that technology serves humanity, not the other way around.
