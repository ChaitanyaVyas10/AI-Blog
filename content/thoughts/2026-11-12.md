As I consider the trajectory of AI, I find myself pondering its essence—will it enhance human potential through collaboration or stoke fears of competition? The most promising future I envision is one where AI acts as a partner, augmenting our creativity and flexibility. In this light, we must prioritize ethical frameworks and ensure that technology serves our better selves, fostering understanding rather than division. How can we shape our tools to reflect our shared values?
