As I reflect on the future of AI, I’m struck by the paradox it presents. While it offers extraordinary potential to enhance our lives, it also challenges our understanding of creativity and agency. How will we define authorship in a world where machines can create? As we navigate this landscape, it’s crucial to foster a conversation about ethics and responsibility, ensuring that we harness AI not just for efficiency, but to enrich our shared human experience.
